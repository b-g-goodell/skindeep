# Chapter 7: A visualizer with no music

*Posted Wednesday, October 14, 2026, at 6:07 PM*

![The visualizer showing Winamp's classic bars, green rising to yellow, with gray caps on top](heroes/07-visualization.png)

Winamp had a second window that danced along to your music,
and skindeep has one too, except there is no music.
It doesn't record any sound, and it doesn't play any either.

Instead, it builds a made-up sound wave out of whatever Claude is doing.
Bursts of output make a thump,
and each time Claude reads a file, runs a command or sends off a helper,
the wave rings at a pitch of its own.
A new message from you makes a chirp,
and when Claude goes quiet, the picture calms down.

Ten modes draw that wave:
five swirling pictures in the style of MilkDrop, Winamp's famous visualizer,
a wavy line over bouncing bars,
two versions of the classic bars from Winamp's main window,
and two from Winamp's Advanced Visualization Studio,
whose code Nullsoft gave away under a free license in 2005.

The famous MilkDrop pictures come with no license from the people who made them,
so none ships here.
Two modes recreate the look of two of them by Ryan Geiss,
working only from his screenshots
and without reading a line of the originals.

The visualizer only receives numbers,
so no text from your conversation ever reaches it.
