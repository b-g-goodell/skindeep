# Chapter 22: Ferns, Go, gravity and a wave

*Posted Wednesday, October 14, 2026, at 11:16 PM*

![Five new visualizer modes side by side: a fern, a Go board, a galaxy with an atom at its center, a star with planets, and a wave passing through two slits](heroes/22-procedural-modes.png)

The visualizer gained five new modes,
each drawn by code, with no pictures or video.

The first grows Barnsley's fern and three of its relatives
from a few rules repeated thousands of times.
The second plays Go on a small board by the real rules,
and each thing Claude does picks the next stone.
The third zooms out through six scenes, each one a detail of the next,
until the galaxy turns out to be the center of an atom and it starts again.
In the fourth, a star, planets and comets pull on each other
and combine when they collide.
The fifth is quantum physics:
a wave flies at a wall with two slits,
and a screen behind it marks where each particle lands.

Each mode follows Claude:
when Claude uses a tool, gravity adds a planet,
and when it finishes, the fern sways in the wind.

At first, the fern and gravity hardly changed at a new message,
since the fern's change took over two seconds
and the new comet started out of view.
Now the old fern dims at once and the star flashes a ring,
and a check confirms that every mode changes at a message.
