# Chapter 18: One pane per tab, and the room's sound

*Posted Wednesday, October 14, 2026, at 9:56 PM*

![The visualizer split into four panes, one per tab, each in its own mode, beside a speaker whose sound feeds in](heroes/18-viz-panes-and-sound.png)

The visualizer showed only the tab you were looking at,
so with three tabs open, two of them went unseen.
Now it splits into one pane per tab, in tab order,
and each pane has its own made-up sound wave, its own mode
and a small label naming its tab and its mode.
A click changes the mode of the pane under the mouse,
and a new pane starts in a mode no other pane is showing.

In chapter 7, the visualizer used no real sound at all,
but then I asked for it to dance to music,
or to anything else the computer plays.
A new menu choice picks what it follows:
the conversation, the computer's sound, or both.
It listens only to what goes out to your speakers, never the microphone,
and keeps only about the last fortieth of a second, saving nothing.

When I closed and reopened the visualizer, it came back black,
and switching modes did nothing.
skindeep's files had been updated while it was running,
so the new window loaded the newer page,
which expected numbers the older running copy never sent.
Now every window skindeep opens later loads the version it started with.
