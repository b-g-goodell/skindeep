# 0031 Fuzz testing with written predictions

Status: accepted 2026-10-13 (maintainer).

## Context

The live checks under `scripts/checks/` each test a path someone thought
of. The maintainer asked for a harness that drives the app with random input,
predicts in writing what each input will do, records what the app did,
and compares the two, so a defect or a harness flaw shows as a prediction
that failed. Its findings are collected in batches.

## Decision

- `scripts/fuzz/model.js`, pure: a seeded generator (mulberry32) of user
  actions and a model of the state the app should show after each, from
  the README and the decisions alone. The actions: New tab and Close tab
  by chord and by menu, Next and Previous tab by chord and by menu,
  Reopen, Duplicate, a drag along the strip, a skin, Show or Hide
  visualization, a visualization mode from the menu, a click and the
  space bar in the visualization, mute, a terminal font size, typing,
  a resize, and a wait while the fake agent works. An action is drawn
  only when the model's state allows it, such as Close tab with two tabs
  or more. The same seed gives the same sequence and predictions.
- A prediction is claims a script checks (the tab ids in order, the
  active tab, one window, the skin, the visualization window and its
  pane count, the active pane's mode when the model knows it or that it
  changed, mute, every terminal's font size, the window's width and the
  skin's scale) and a sentence for the screenshot's reviewer. Any page
  or console error of any window is a mismatch too.
- `scripts/fuzz/run.js OUT --seed N --steps N --skin NAME --agent
  claude|codex` writes `predictions.md` and `.json` and their sha256 in
  `run.json` before the app starts, then runs the steps under
  `scripts/drive.js` with `scripts/fake` first on PATH, so no real CLI,
  model call or sound is involved. Each step records both windows'
  screenshots and the observed state. Mismatches go to `findings.md`
  and `.json` with the seed, the step, the prediction, the observation
  and the screenshots.
- The model does not follow the app after a mismatch, so a later
  finding may only repeat an earlier one. Each finding names the first
  mismatch of its run.
- A finding is `unreviewed` until a reviewer opens its screenshots and
  classes it: a defect of the app, a harness flaw, or a wrong prediction.
  The script checks the claims. A person or an agent looks at the
  pictures.
- `--until K` replays a seed to step K. `--break next-tab` runs a copy
  of the app whose menu Next tab goes to the previous tab, to show the
  harness finds a defect: seed 23 over 40 steps finds it at step 4.

## Why

Predictions written before the run cannot be fitted to its outcome. A
model built from the documents, not the code, catches a document and
the app disagreeing in either direction. Drawing actions from the
model's state keeps a run from ending the app at its second step.

## Rejected

- Predictions written by hand per run: they would not scale to many
  seeds, and the findings come in batches.
- Re-syncing the model to the app after a mismatch: it would hide the
  wrong state from every later step's claims, and the first finding
  already names the cause to look at.
- Pop-out among the actions: a second window splits the keyboard
  and the probe between windows. `scripts/checks/popout.js` covers it.
